The bank prices collateral. RAIL prices verified cash flow.

RAIL verifies export receipts, platform payouts and patents as on-chain evidence, issues a Credit Passport — evidence, score, limit, APR — and tokenizes approved receivables as ERC-3643 RWAs on Base. Four agents collect, verify, underwrite and broker; a deterministic engine computes every number.
